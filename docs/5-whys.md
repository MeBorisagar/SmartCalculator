## 5 Whys Analysis 

### Problem
Customers are abandoning the loan form on step 2 without completing it.

### Whys

Why are customers abandoning the form on step 2?

- Because they stop filling out the required information before submitting the application.


Why?

- Because they find the questions on step 2 confusing and are unsure what information is required.

Why?

- Because the form provides little explanation or guidance for the required fields.


Why?

- Because the feature was designed without sufficient focus on the user experience and field-level(domain) help.

Why?

- Because customer feedback and usability testing were not conducted before releasing the feature.



### Root Cause & Fix

- Conduct usability testing with customers before each release and improve the form by adding field descriptions, examples, and validation messages based on the feedback received.
